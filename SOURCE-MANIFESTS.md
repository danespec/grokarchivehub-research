## What a hash verifies

A SHA-256 digest can help identify whether two available files have identical bytes. It **does not** independently authenticate authorship, establish truth, verify that an allegation is correct, or make a private source file public.

## Source material policy

This repository does not host PDFs, screenshots, scans, private raw email archives, signatures, financial ledgers, media tranches or personal information. Follow canonical GAH publication links and government/court URLs where available. Never submit confidential source documents through a public GitHub issue.

## Original record versus interpretation

Always distinguish a dated record, its author's assertion, GAH's interpretation, and what remains unverified. These categories are especially important for allegations about identifiable people, victims and financial transactions.
