## Evidence boundaries

- **[New DOJ Release Documents March 2025 Epstein File Review Instructions](https://grokarchivehub.com/news/doj-march-2025-review-emails-released):** Flagging public figures for review is not proof of hiding records.
- **[Judge Orders Redacted FBI Notes for Private Review by October 16](https://grokarchivehub.com/news/phang-fbi-notes-in-camera-october-16):** October 16 in-camera deadline is not an order to publish the notes that day.
- **[Why a Chinese Consortium Declined a Proposed $1.6 Billion Oil Deal](https://grokarchivehub.com/news/gadeco-chinese-consortium-declined):** The offer and broker fee proposals have no established closing or payment.
