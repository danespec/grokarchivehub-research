## Unresolved claims matter

- **[DOJ's March 2025 Epstein Review Emails: Public-Figure Flags, Victim Protections and the Missing Plan](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/doj-oip-epstein-review-public-figure-flags-2026)** — March 2025 review instructions and flagged names are not proof of withholding.
- **[FBI Vault Part 13: March Redaction Orders, July Statement Emails, and the Phang Case](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/fbi-vault-redaction-chronology-phang)** — 2025 FBI redaction records must not be conflated with later court proceedings.
- **[Wyden’s Missing Banker 302 vs. Three Public Deutsche Interviews](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/wyden-banker-302-deutsche-interviews)** — Three public Deutsche Bank-linked FD-302s do not identify Wyden's unnamed missing banker.
- **[Nine SARs, $1.28 Billion, and the Reporting Gap in Epstein’s JPMorgan Accounts](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/jpmorgan-nine-sars-epstein)** — Suspicious-activity reports are transaction reporting, not findings of criminal guilt.
- **[December 6–7, 2007: The Victim Letters, the Defense Papers, and the Missing Decision Record](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/epstein-december-6-7-2007-decision-gap)** — The public record does not establish the decision maker behind the victim-letter hold.
- **[Why the Chinese Consortium Declined the GADECO Deal: Missing Accounts and a $1.6 Billion Valuation](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/barak-gadeco-fee-2014)** — A proposed $1.6B acquisition and contingent fees are not a verified sale or payment.
- **[A Proposed $200 Million Saudi–Israeli Healthcare Fund, Documented but Not Funded](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/barak-saudi-rothschild-proposal-2015)** — A 2015 proposed fund is not evidence that the vehicle was financed or launched.
- **[Barak's 2015 Putin Meeting Request: An Official Refusal, Not a Completed Appointment](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/barak-ushakov-2013-2015)** — A documented refused appointment is not proof of an earlier or completed meeting.
- **[Fourteen Barak Archive Leads: Verified Documents, Corrections and Open Receipts](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/barak-14-leads-evidence-index)** — Leads and records carry different proof levels; the index distinguishes proposed from completed.
- **[Børge Brende & Epstein: Norway’s 2017 Email Question](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/norway-brende-epstein-hearing-2026)** — 2017 references and the claimed first meeting require separate corroboration.
- **[George Mitchell in the Epstein Records: What Resolves Cleanly—and What Does Not](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/george-mitchell-epstein-source-audit)** — Namesakes, allegations, denials and independent contact records are separate evidence classes.
- **[The Andriesz Paper Trail: FBI Hits, CFTC, HMRC and FCA Review](https://grokarchivehub.com/investigations/andriesz-adfin-lutnick-paper-trail)** — Separate regulator proceedings cannot be merged into a single causal finding.
