# Grok Archive Hub — Public Research Index

**A citable directory of published investigations and short, source-linked news reports.**
Grok Archive Hub (GAH) is an independent investigative newsroom and evidence archive: https://grokarchivehub.com

This GitHub repository is a **navigation and provenance index**, not a mirrored evidence dump or the website's production source code. The linked GAH pages are the canonical published versions and remain responsible for updates and corrections.

## Coverage and maintenance

This is a **curated initial selection of 12 investigations and 3 news briefs**, not a count of all GAH publications or underlying files. Metadata and links were checked against public article pages on 2026-10-12. Additional records will be added only after editorial and privacy review.

Source-manifest links may expose source identifiers and cryptographic digests; they do not necessarily expose the underlying material. No source PDF, private correspondence, bank record, signature, victim identifying information, unreleased source file, or private working directory is included here.

No affiliation or endorsement by any government agency or another research site is implied. This is not a link-exchange scheme. External sites and source records retain their own terms and rights.
