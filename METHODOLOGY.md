# Research standards and verification

This index follows the public-facing methodology at https://grokarchivehub.com/methodology, https://grokarchivehub.com/editorial-policy and https://grokarchivehub.com/corrections.

1. **Open the publication.** Confirm headline, visible publication date, last update and canonical URL.
2. **Trace a specific claim.** Locate the cited official release, court document, public source ledger or contemporary original.
3. **Preserve evidence categories.** A name in an email, proposed transaction, possible meeting, court allegation, regulatory action and criminal finding are different things.
4. **Check provenance.** Note source document date, release date, page or record ID, redactions and contemporaneous context. OCR and extracted snippets can contain errors.
5. **Look for what is missing.** The article should distinguish supported claims from unverified transactions, identities, intent and outcomes.
6. **Report corrections.** Use the published editorial corrections channel; don't upload confidential material to GitHub.

**Examples from this catalog:**

- DOJ public-figure review flags are **not proof** of deletion or concealment.
- An *in camera* judicial review order is **not** an order to publicly release redacted notes.
- A contingent brokerage commission or engineering estimate is **not** evidence of a closed and paid oil acquisition.
- An FBI interview reference and an anticipated proffer do **not** prove a missing banker's identity or that a proffer occurred.
